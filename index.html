Who Am I

Professional Background

I am Eng. Mohsen Elwan, a Public Health Pest Control Specialist and Field Operations Manager. I have extensive experience planning and executing IPM programs across diverse sites, including luxury residential complexes, commercial facilities, and large-scale projects.

I firmly believe that effective pest control begins with understanding the source of the problem, not simply rushing to apply pesticides. This philosophy forms the foundation of my work and is the core message of my book, "The Beginning".
